# Interactive circularity rehearsal

Run the README setup and choose your own local demo password. Every person below must sign in with their own explicitly provisioned account; roles are not unrestricted demo identities. Sign out between different people. Fictional account prefixes are listed in README.

1. **originator**: inspect `CS-BLR-002` with unknown chemistry/mass, and create/screen intake with missing authority evidence. **reviewer** records evidence review separately. **originator2** has the held swelling battery; ordinary listing remains blocked.
2. **originator / buyer**: publish a screened battery, submit an eligible offer, select it, and separately confirm the same terms version. Inspect incompatible receiver exclusions.
3. **transporter / buyer**: submit an above-cap quote, observe booking blocked, propose cap amendment or another quote, reconfirm revised terms when needed. Generate direct and one-hub battery route alternatives and view textual route details.
4. Book only after quote acceptance and capacity reservation. **originator / transporter** each confirm physical pickup. **transporter** records transit/arrival and labelled synthetic telemetry. **buyer** records receipt/inspection. A discrepancy needs reviewer resolution; custody does not move on arrival alone.
5. Record-only payment needs buyer report and originator confirmation. Keep disposition separate from receipt. Close the first coordination transaction only after all original gates are satisfied.
6. **buyer**, Circular supply chain → Assessments: assign the physically received battery to `fac-refurb` for REFURBISHMENT. **technician** submits its method/equipment/measurements, scope, limitations and evidence. **reviewer** independently reviews; submission alone is not authoritative.
7. **buyer**, Operations: link the held battery at `fac-refurb`, create a REFURBISHMENT job. **originator** explicitly approves operation and output-title terms. **buyer** starts, reports a uniquely identified PRODUCT `REFURBISHED_PACK`, measured output mass and loss totaling input mass. **reviewer** accepts reported processing. **buyer** records application-specific product acceptance.
8. **buyer**, Downstream handoffs: propose sale to `fac-second`, known price/cap/payer, receiving specification and evidence. **secondlife** and **buyer** confirm the exact version. **transporter** sees an eligible operational quote request and submits its own quote. **secondlife** books. Sender and carrier separately confirm pickup; carrier records arrival; receiver records inspected receipt; buyer/seller separately report/confirm payment record; receiver closes.
9. **secondlife**: integrate the received pack in a job at `fac-second`, approve its now-owned input, report a `SECOND_LIFE_SYSTEM` PRODUCT with measured quantities and source links. Review and accept; transfer to `fac-customer` through the same commercial/physical workflow.
10. **customer**: record deployment at its own organization, service and retirement. Retirement does not change physical custody. Propose onward transfer of the retired system to `fac-recycle` under explicit receiving requirements; complete its actual handoff.
11. **recycler**: create RECYCLING job from the received retired product. Multiple MATERIAL outputs (e.g. BLACK_MASS/COPPER) plus documented loss reconcile to input mass. A recycling batch retains input links; arriving at a recycler did not itself mean recycling happened.
12. Transfer black mass to **refiner**, process at `fac-refiner`, document measured `REFINED_LITHIUM`, transfer to **manufacturer**, process at `fac-manufacturer`, and inspect the final lot's provenance trace back through all jobs to the original battery. Blended inputs are supported without a claim of exact atomic allocation.
13. Pathways: compare complete qualified stage chains using explicit economic assumptions. Leave a cost blank to demonstrate incomplete economics. Add known synthetic amounts to rank conditional estimates. Inspect missing assessments/output acceptance, failed scope/capacity and preserved decision history. A recommendation has no reservation or execution side effect.

For the reproducible automated version run `python integration_smoke.py` at the root with the environment activated. It uses an isolated temporary database and executes this chain through real servers. Browser interaction and layout should still be checked in the target browser.

Potential safety incidents retain HOLD/manual qualified-review tasks. Ordinary transformations and downstream booking cannot bypass held products. No actual money, messages, safety certifications or live hardware are simulated as real.
