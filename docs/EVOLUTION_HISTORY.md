## Decisions and remaining requirements

- **No browser visual/interaction QA or public deployment** was performed. Real HTTP integration, module serving, type checks and build are separate verified checks.
- No email infrastructure exists. Registration activation and recovery delivery are administrator-mediated; automatic verified-email onboarding, invitations and email recovery are not implemented. Initial membership requires platform identity/consent review. Initial joining can be approved through the platform membership form after recorded identity/consent review.
- Reviewers have explicit **GLOBAL_OPERATIONS** documentary scope. Fine-grained per-case/regional assignment is not implemented. Platform account administration is separate and cannot be granted through organization roles.
- Production mode sets Secure cookies and rejects synthetic accounts/organizations/operational qualifications. HTTPS hosting/proxy/CSP, monitoring, MFA, backup encryption, email delivery, upload malware scanning and external permit authenticity checks remain deployment work. Limits and SQLite locks target one instance; expired session/attempt housekeeping is manual.
- Qualified assessment and processing review record documentary acceptance; neither software nor mock seed verification certifies safety. A new product incident retains HOLD and a manual qualified-review task. Controlled redirection/incident-resolution of existing battery or downstream shipments is not automated.
- Pathway templates are conditional plans, not guaranteed technical recipes. Actual composition, yield, output specification, later-stage schedule and receiving acceptance must be documented at execution. Economic inputs are explicit owner estimates/synthetic assumptions; no live market benchmarks, complete-pathway external quote import or globally optimized fleet scheduling is implemented.
- Downstream product/material transport supports direct routes with verified scopes and serialized capacity. Original battery routes retain direct/one-hub planning and approved manifests. Independent carriers per hub leg, downstream hub consolidation and joint batch discounts are not implemented; savings remain unknown without an approved comparable baseline.
- Transformations consume entire linked items. Multiple inputs/outputs are normalized, with documented process loss. Partial lot splitting/consumption, arbitrary added integration components and exact allocation of blended material atoms are not implemented. Additional mass must be explicitly represented, not invented.
- Product take-back requires both parties to confirm the same terms. Contract amendment/version UI, warranty accounting, detailed invoice/tax/instalment accounting, automatic scheduling and long-term hardware monitoring remain extensions. Settlement records reports/confirmations and never transfers money.
- Original disposition attestations remain clearly self-reported records for compatibility. New processing jobs add normalized measured output/review lineage; closing the first commercial transaction does not close the downstream circular supply chain.
- Optional manufacturer/model/serial/electrical/history/BMS passport fields now persist as typed intake metadata. Existing battery columns are reused; no duplicate battery table was introduced. Dimensions are validated at the API; richer dedicated dimension/service-history editors remain limited.

Known unresolved test failures: **none in the executed final checks**. One test-client deprecation warning remains.

The idempotent `python -m app.seed` command also succeeded against the existing local database after the v2 migration; no reset was used.
